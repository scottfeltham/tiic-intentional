The people

Who you will meet

Three of ten named in the deck. On the live site this stays switched off until each person has given written consent and supplied a photograph.

  • Portrait — supplied by Aarti Shah

    Aarti Shah

    Powerlifter, TEDx speaker, dancer

    Born with Down syndrome, she represents the UAE internationally. A diagnosis does not define what a person can achieve.

  • Portrait — supplied by Adbhut

    Adbhut

    Musicians of Hope

    The UAE’s first professionally trained band of People of Determination. Paid work and real careers, not a good cause.

  • Portrait — supplied by Mahek Mansukhlal

    Mahek Mansukhlal

    Designer, creator of HiSign

    An educational game born from her own experience as a deaf person, bringing Deaf and hearing people together through play.
