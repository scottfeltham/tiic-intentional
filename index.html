Our vision

More than one afternoon

The beginning of a community dedicated to celebrating life and creating opportunity across the UAE. We expect around eight hundred guests.

The day is for People of Determination, neurodivergent people, the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community, people with visual impairments, people with Down syndrome and developmental conditions, older adults, older adults living with memory conditions, cancer survivors, people living with chronic illness, people living with mental health challenges, people in recovery, caregivers, educators, healthcare professionals, volunteers, and every family in the UAE.

Together, we can build a more inclusive, accepting and empowered world for all.
